Written by Jarin Wadiwalla for the AMI 12–18 Diploma Course at The Montessori Centre for Work and Study in Sätila, Sweden. Originally written in March 2024 and updated in April 2026, this paper reviews the research on adolescent social media use — from developmental neuroscience to sleep, mental health, and family media plans — and closes with practical approaches for supporting adolescents in forming healthy online habits.
